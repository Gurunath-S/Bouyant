# Iconography Rules: No Sparkles / AI Sparkle Icons

## Rule: Prohibit `Sparkles` / AI Sparkle Badges
- **NEVER** use `Sparkles`, `Sparkle`, or AI magic-wand/sparkle icons in any UI component, page, badge, or button.
- AI sparkles look cliché, gimmicky, and out of place in professional business software.

## Replacement Icons
Always use semantically clear, domain-specific icons:
- For suggestions, regeneration, or refreshing: use `RotateCw` or `RefreshCw`.
- For booths, floor plans, and layouts: use `LayoutGrid`, `Layers`, or `LayoutTemplate`.
- For metrics, streams, and recent events: use `Activity`, `TrendingUp`, or `Calendar`.
- For exploration, discovery, and navigation: use `Compass` or `Search`.
- For staff workspaces: use `Briefcase` or `ShieldCheck`.
- For presets or media: use `ImageIcon` or `BookmarkCheck`.
